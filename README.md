# Micro-Credentials Registry

This is the main repository for `Micro-Credentials` designed by our `Open Community`. 

## Micro-Credential

A micro-credential is issued to a `holder` as a form of recognition for their achievement of a specific skill, knowledge area, or competency. The main features of a micro-credential are as follows:

### Short Duration

Completed in days, weeks, or a few months instead of years.

### Competency-based

Earned by demonstrating an actual skill through assessments.

### Verifiable

Issued via digital credentials that are cryptographically secure and can be verified by anyone without contacting the issuer directly.

### Stackable

Designed to accumulate over time, allowing holders to combine multiple micro-credentials into a full macro-credential.

## Open Community

An open community is a public group or collaborative space where anyone can join, view content, and participate freely without needing formal approvals, invitations, or paid memberships.

## Sectors and Functional Areas

A `sector` is a large grouping of jobs, businesses, and economic activities categorized by the primary type of work, service, or product they produce.  For example, a programmer will work within the `Telecommunications, computer programming, consultancy, computing infrastructure, and other information
service activities` sector.

A sector is further subdivided into `Functional Groups`. A functional group represents a role that perform the same core business function or specialized operational tasks. For example, `Other computer programming activities` is a functional group within the `Telecommunications, computer programming, consultancy, computing infrastructure, and other information
service activities` sector.

Sectors and Functional Groups classifications are in accordance with the following standard:

[ International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC) Rev. 5](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/ISIC_Rev5_Publication-white_cover_version.pdf)

## Issuers

An issuer will issue a digital credential in the [W3C Verifiable Credential (VC)](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-overview-1.0/) format. An issuer must first provide evidence to validate they are who they claim to be. Issuers can apply for approval in the [VC Trust Registry](https://trustregistry.vc.rclapp.com/).

## How it Works

### Register to Contribute

Experts in a functional group will register to contribute to the development of the micro-credentials. The registration form is provided below:

[Team Member Registration Form]()

### Contributing to a Micro-Credential

Each functional group, has a development `Team` that that comprise of `Team Members` who have expertise in the functional area(s). Any team member can edit an existing credential or add a new credential. The team will come to a consensus on the acceptance of changes to a credential. The team will have a member appointed as a `Team Maintainer` for the functional group. The team will identify a strategy for updating the credentials and releasing official versions.

### Creating a new Sector, Functional Group or Micro-Credential

Any team member or individual can open a request in the the [Issues](https://github.com/micro-credentials/main/issues) section and describe the new sector, functional group or micro-credential to be developed. Once approval is granted the development work can commence. The issues page to request the development of a new item is provided below:

[Create and Issue](https://github.com/micro-credentials/main/issues)
